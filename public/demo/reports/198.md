## Final diagnosis

**Uterus, cervix, bilateral fallopian tubes and ovaries, total abdominal hysterectomy and bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy:**
- Endometrium: weakly proliferative endometrium, negative for hyperplasia and malignancy
- Myometrium: adenomyosis
- Cervix: chronic cervicitis and nabothian cysts
- Left ovary: corpus luteum cyst; left fallopian tube: paratubal cyst
