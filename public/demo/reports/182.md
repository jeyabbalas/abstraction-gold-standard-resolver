## Final diagnosis

**A. Uterus, cervix, bilateral fallopian tubes, parametria and vaginal cuff, radical hysterectomy and bilateral salpingectomy:**
- Invasive squamous cell carcinoma of the cervix, keratinizing, 2.6 cm
- Depth of stromal invasion 7 mm of 15 mm cervical wall thickness
- Lymphovascular invasion: present
- Tumor involves the ectocervix and endocervix; lower uterine segment not involved
- High-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion (CIN 3) adjacent to the carcinoma
- P16 immunohistochemistry: diffuse block-type positivity
- Parametria and vaginal cuff margin negative for carcinoma; closest margin (vaginal cuff) 1.2 cm
- Endometrium: proliferative phase endometrium
- Myometrium: adenomyosis
- Bilateral fallopian tubes: no pathologic abnormality
