## Final diagnosis

**A. Uterus, cervix, bilateral fallopian tubes and ovaries, total laparoscopic hysterectomy and bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy:**
- Serous carcinoma of the endometrium, 3.6 cm
- Tumor invades 12 mm into a 17 mm thick myometrium (71%)
- Lymphovascular invasion: present
- Cervical stroma not involved
- Bilateral ovaries and fallopian tubes: no tumor identified

**B. Omentum, omentectomy:**
- Metastatic serous carcinoma, multiple foci, largest 1.5 cm

**C. Right pelvic sentinel lymph node, excision:**
- One lymph node, negative for metastatic carcinoma (0/1)
- Negative by ultrastaging (H&E levels and cytokeratin immunohistochemistry)

**D. Left pelvic sentinel lymph node, excision:**
- One lymph node, negative for metastatic carcinoma (0/1)
